Hi! I'm Mithali Iyer, a senior studying Information Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I am also pursuing a minor in Computer Science and a minor in Advertising. I am passionate about User Experience Design and Human Computer Interaction. I currently work with Professor Rachel Adler as an undergraduate researcher in the Information Experience and Accessibility Lab at the iSchool.

I am also part of a club called Design for America, and am a Project Fellow for HerWorld!, a project under Illinois Enactus. This past summer, I worked as an Earthmoving UX Design Intern at John Deere, where I designed frameworks for improving the experience of displays on construction equipment for operators.
